Vehicle Financing Options

Compare dealership-arranged financing through Westlake Financial with the option to try PenFed Credit Union for an auto loan.

Choose How to Arrange Financing

Financing through Marshall Auto

Marshall Auto works with Westlake Financial for dealership-arranged vehicle financing. Contact our team to ask about the lender's credit application process and the vehicle you are considering. Westlake Financial makes its own credit decision and sets any offered terms.

Approval is not guaranteed. Eligibility and any down payment, annual percentage rate (APR), loan term, amount financed, and payment amount depend on the applicant, the vehicle, lender requirements, and final underwriting. An application or preliminary indication is not a commitment to lend; only the final signed credit agreement and associated disclosures establish the binding terms.

Try PenFed Credit Union

You may also try PenFed Credit Union for an auto loan. This is a separate application that is not submitted or decided by Marshall Auto. PenFed determines eligibility, approval, and loan terms; check directly with PenFed about the vehicle you are considering. Approval is not guaranteed.

Explore PenFed Auto Loans

Check with the lender about the specific vehicle, including its title status, before applying. Do not assume that a lender will finance every vehicle or title classification.

  • Dealership-arranged financing applications are reviewed by Westlake Financial
  • You may compare lender offers and choose whether to proceed
  • Trade-in value may be discussed as part of the purchase; it does not guarantee a particular loan payment

Before You Apply or Sign

  • Ask the lender which documents and information it requires; requirements vary.
  • Review any credit-report authorization and application disclosures before submitting information.
  • Compare the APR, amount financed, finance charge, total of payments, payment schedule, and any required down payment or fees in the written offer.
  • Confirm insurance availability and coverage requirements for the specific vehicle, especially if it has a branded title.
  • Do not send account numbers, identity documents, or other sensitive financial information through our general contact form or email. Ask us for the lender's authorized application channel.
