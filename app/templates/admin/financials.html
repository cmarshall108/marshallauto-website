How these numbers are counted
  • Closed-deal profit is booked in the month the vehicle sold, using Eastern Time.
  • Sale amounts are out-the-door. Kept revenue removes the 3% highway-use tax, because that tax is remitted. A saved receipt supplies the split; otherwise the same split is estimated from the amount received.
  • Acquisition and prep costs of a sold car reduce that sale month, even if the receipts are older. The per-vehicle rebuild tracker still shows profit before this tax split.
  • Cash expense bars use the purchase date on each cost item and include cars still in inventory. Undated costs are listed separately.
  • Doc, warranty, and tag fees are added only to contribution, not to vehicle gross profit. Trade allowances are shown, but not subtracted, because the trade-in vehicle is not valued here.
