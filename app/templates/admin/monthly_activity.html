Website analytics

These are separate activity signals, not a count of individual buyers. Page views include vehicle views; contact clicks do not confirm a conversation. Leads include all non-spam sources, not only vehicle inquiries. Other tracked events include searches, filters and engagement.

Counts begin with the first recorded observation for each signal; earlier months are unknown (—), not zero. Tracking gaps, blocked scripts, bots, staff scans and unrecorded offline activity can affect results. QR tracking requires newly printed tracked codes.

Seasonal averages include complete months after each signal's first recorded month; the current month is excluded. Sample counts show how many years contribute. Sales require a stored sale date; {{ undated_sales }} currently sold vehicle(s) have no date and are excluded.
