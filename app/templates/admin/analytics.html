How this works
Viewer counts Each public page load sends an anonymous page view with path, page type, device, and acquisition tags. Bots are filtered out of reports.
Time & interest Heartbeats update time-on-page and scroll depth. Clicks to call/text, inventory filters, gallery use, payment calculator, CarFax downloads, and lead submits are stored as interest events.
Privacy Visitor IDs are random browser IDs (not names or emails). IP addresses are one-way hashed. Admin pages and static assets are never tracked.
